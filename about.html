My Story

Where I come from
and why I do this.

Every VA has a "why." Here's mine unfiltered and honest.

2021 — Age 13

First Step Into the Online World

My brother introduced me to Web3 and crypto during the pandemic. He managed 10 NFT game accounts across 5 clients and gave me one to run a strategic play-to-earn game. That was my first real taste of earning online and working with responsibility at 13 years old.

2022 — Grade 9

Real Online Work Begins — DaoLabs

My teacher introduced me to DaoLabs, a Web3 community platform where I earned by completing real tasks writing content about projects and designing visual banners. Every output I submitted was reviewed and rewarded with points I could withdraw as real cash. My first real freelance experience.

2025 — Grade 11

TVL-ICT, OJT & Research Leadership

I chose TVL-ICT and joined the top class with high grades. During my 10-day OJT I serviced laptops, desktop units, printers, and network devices and earned the highest performance rating in my batch. That same year I led my class research team and built a QR code-based attendance system using self-taught coding and AI tools. I was the only student who developed a working tech-based solution.

Early 2026

A Defining Moment That Changed Everything

A deeply personal challenge in early 2026 shifted my perspective completely. I stopped drifting and started building. I began planning every day with intention reflecting each night, waking up with purpose, and choosing growth over comfort. That shift is what drove everything that came after.

2026 — Today

NC2 Certified. Portfolio Built. Ready.

I passed the TESDA NC2 Computer Systems Servicing assessment as the sole qualifier out of 27 classmates. I returned to DaoLabs in Grade 12, built this portfolio website from scratch, and am now actively building a VA career I'm proud of — with full transparency, real skills, and a genuine hunger to grow.
